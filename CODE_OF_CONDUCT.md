# Community Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful learning community.

## Expected behavior

- Be patient with beginners and respectful of different backgrounds.
- Give specific, constructive feedback.
- Welcome questions, corrections, and different learning speeds.
- Focus criticism on code, explanations, or ideas—not personal traits.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, and repeated disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions that violate these standards. Concerns should be raised privately with project maintainers when possible.
